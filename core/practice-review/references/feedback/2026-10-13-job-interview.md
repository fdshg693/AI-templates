## 要点

- 期間は `for 5 years`。`since` の後ろは起点（`since 2021`）。
- `good at` の後ろは名詞か動名詞（`good at communicating`）。
- `discuss` は他動詞。`about` を付けない。

## 直すところ

| 言ったこと | 直し | ポイント | 出典 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I have worked at ABC company since 5 years. | I have worked at ABC for five years. | 期間は `for`、起点は `since` | [2026-10-12-job-interview-self-introduction](../sessions/2026/2026-10-12-job-interview-self-introduction.md) |
| I'm good at to communicate with team members. | I'm good at communicating with my team. | 前置詞の後ろは動名詞 | 同上 |
| We discussed about the release schedule. | We discussed the release schedule. | `discuss` は目的語を直接とる | 同上 |
| I suggested to split the release into two. | I suggested splitting the release into two. | `suggest` の後ろは動名詞か that 節 | 同上 |
