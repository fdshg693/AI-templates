# Ordering coffee practice

#### You:

Let's practice ordering coffee at a cafe. You are the staff.

#### ChatGPT:

Sure! Hi there, welcome in. What can I get started for you today?

#### You:

Can I get a one latte, please?

#### ChatGPT:

Of course! What size would you like — small, medium, or large?

#### You:

Medium. And how much is it cost?

#### ChatGPT:

A medium latte is $4.50. Is that for here or to go?

#### You:

I want to drink here.

#### ChatGPT:

Great, for here. Can I get a name for the order?
