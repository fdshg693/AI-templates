# Job interview self-introduction

#### You:

Please be an interviewer. I want to practice a self-introduction for a software job.

#### ChatGPT:

Happy to. Let's begin. Could you tell me a little about yourself?

#### You:

I have worked at ABC company since 5 years. I'm good at to communicate with team members.

#### ChatGPT:

Thank you. Could you give me an example of a time you resolved a disagreement in your team?

#### You:

We discussed about the release schedule. I suggested to split the release into two, and everyone agreed.

#### ChatGPT:

That's a good example. What did you learn from that experience?

#### You:

I learned that it is important to listen first. Thank you for your time.
